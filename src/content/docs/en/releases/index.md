## Upgrade notes

- Restart the service after upgrading.
- The legacy `~/.benchscope/config.json` migrates automatically to `settings.json` — no manual step needed.
- Back up the data root directory before upgrading (see [Install → Update & Uninstall](/en/docs/install/update-uninstall/)).
